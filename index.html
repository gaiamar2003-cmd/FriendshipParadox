Why do your friends have more friends than you?

1. The hat game

20 people. Each line is a friendship. Bigger circle = more friends.
Pick someone at random, then look at one of their friends. Who has more friends?

Press Pick someone, or tap a person on the graph.

Why does this happen?

A popular person is the friend of many people. So when you pick someone and look at one of their friends, you very often land on a popular person. Unpopular people are the friend of only a few, so they rarely show up as "the friend". That is why your friends, on average, have more friends than you.

Why are small dots red and big dots green?

A small dot usually has at least one friend who is a big dot, a very popular student. That popular friend pulls up the average, so the small dot's friends have more friends than it: red. A big dot is the popular one itself, so it stays green.
